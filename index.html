About This Lab

The Maryville University AI Lab is a hands-on learning and research hub for students and faculty. All AI projects are created by faculty and the lab apprentice and are intended as educational resources for students to explore AI, Machine Learning, Data Science, and Computer Vision through practical examples and research initiatives.
